        BATTLE REFERENCE

        FIGHT and Equipment Rules

        A quick reference for weapon rolls, damage soak, position, Defend, and party Star Points.

        How a FIGHT resolves
        1. Roll 1d20. Meet the weapon Hit Number plus target SPD divided by 4, rounded down. Natural 20 hits and natural 1 misses.
        2. Use the weapon’s hit count, including its critical rule.
        3. Roll damage for each hit and add total ATK once.
        4. Reduce every landed hit by target DEF divided by 4, rounded down, minimum 1 damage per hit.
        Position, Defend, and Star Gauge

        Most weapons require Near. Ranged can hit Far. Defend halves every hit until the next turn. A shield makes the first Defend reduce damage by three quarters, but consecutive shield Defends become normal half damage. The Star Gauge gains 2 marks at the start of a round, 1 for a critical hit, full dodge, or Defend, and 2 for Protect. At 10 marks, the party gains 5 SP, up to 9 SP.
